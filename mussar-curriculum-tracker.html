Savlanut — Patience

סַבְלָנוּת

Bearing the burden of uncomfortable, frustrating, or delayed situations without reacting with anger or resentment. It is the conscious choice to take a deep breath, remain present, and accept what you cannot control. The root of savlanut means both "to suffer" and "to carry a load" — impatience is the heavy bag; patience is learning to carry it with grace.
